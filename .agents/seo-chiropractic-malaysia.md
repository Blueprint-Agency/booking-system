## Key takeaways

1. **Recurring winners:** chiropractic-in-malaysia.com, mychiro.com.my, renewchiropractic.com.my rank across multiple keywords — all use the same playbook: location-specific service pages (`/back-pain-treatment-kuala-lumpur/`) + condition blogs.
2. **Condition keywords beat category keywords for opportunity.** "back pain treatment malaysia" SERP is weak (Indian + US sites at #2–3); a well-built local condition page can hit page-1 quickly. "slipped disc" is harder (hospital domains own it) but chiros still take spots 6–8.
3. **Pricing content is the biggest gap.** PAA begs for "chiropractic cost in Malaysia" content; ranking sites mostly hide prices. A transparent pricing page/guide is a high-probability featured-snippet play.
4. **Local pack = reviews.** Winners have 90–350 Google reviews at 4.7–5.0★; GBP review velocity matters as much as the website. One competitor (Renew) is buying sponsored local-pack placement.
5. **"Vs physiotherapy" and "is it legit/recognised in MY" comparison content** matches live PAA demand and is underserved by local sites.
6. **Penang is the softest market** — an irrelevant US blog ranks #1; thin local content overall.
